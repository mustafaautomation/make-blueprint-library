# Social

Portfolio demos by Mustafa ([mustafaautomation](https://github.com/mustafaautomation)). Not client work.

Turn one source into channel-ready posts.

| Scenario | Difficulty | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Competitor changelog RSS to Slack](competitor-changelog-rss/) | beginner | Two sentences on a competitor changelog item, plus the link. |
| [Content calendar row to a caption](content-calendar-caption/) | beginner | Write a caption into column B when a content idea is added to column A. |
| [Long note into a LinkedIn draft and a short post](thread-to-linkedin-and-short/) | intermediate | Turn a long note in column A into two drafts in column B. |
| [Repurpose an RSS article into LinkedIn, Facebook, and X](social-repurpose-channels/) | intermediate | Turn a new RSS item into three channel posts and log the copy before anything publishes. |
| [Social mention webhook to Slack](mention-webhook-to-slack/) | beginner | Log a mention and post the text. Do not auto-reply. |
| [Testimonial cell to a one-sentence quote](testimonial-to-quote-card/) | beginner | Compress a testimonial into one sentence that does not add praise. |
| [Weekly social stats note](weekly-social-stats-note/) | beginner | Describe the social stat rows marked YES. No growth advice. |
| [YouTube RSS item to show notes](youtube-rss-show-notes/) | beginner | Turn a new video title and description into three show-note bullets. |
