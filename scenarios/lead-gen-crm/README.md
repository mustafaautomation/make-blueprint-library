# Lead gen and CRM

Portfolio demos by Mustafa ([mustafaautomation](https://github.com/mustafaautomation)). Not client work.

Capture a lead, score or enrich it, and write it to a CRM.

| Scenario | Difficulty | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI lead capture, score, and HubSpot contact](ai-lead-score-hubspot/) | intermediate | Score a new lead from a form webhook, create the HubSpot contact when they are warm, and draft email for the middle of the list. |
| [AI lead score into a Pipedrive person and deal](ai-lead-pipedrive/) | intermediate | Create a Pipedrive person from a scored lead and open a deal only when the score clears 65. |
| [AI lead score into GoHighLevel and a text](ai-lead-gohighlevel/) | intermediate | Score a webhook lead, upsert the contact in GoHighLevel, and text them when the score clears 75. |
| [Airtable CRM record summary](airtable-crm-ai-summary/) | intermediate | When an Airtable CRM record changes, write a short AI summary back onto the record and ping Slack if the stage is won. |
| [Booth lead with a badge email](event-booth-lead/) | beginner | Create a HubSpot contact from a booth scan only when the badge had an email. |
| [Pricing-page question into a draft](pricing-page-chat/) | intermediate | Draft a reply when a pricing-page question names a seat count. |
| [Qualify a chat widget transcript into HubSpot](chat-widget-qualify-hubspot/) | intermediate | Read a chat transcript and create a HubSpot contact only when an email and a need are both present. |
| [Score a partner referral before a draft goes out](partner-referral-score/) | intermediate | Draft a thank-you to the partner when the referral includes a way to contact the buyer. |
| [Sheet row of a prospect to an outreach draft](linkedin-row-outreach-draft/) | intermediate | Draft a four-sentence outreach note into column B from the prospect in column A. |
| [Webinar registration scored into HubSpot](webinar-registration-score/) | intermediate | Create a HubSpot contact for a webinar registration that names a job title. |
